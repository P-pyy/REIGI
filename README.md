## 🔴 `> ABOUT THE PROJECT`

**REIGI (Registrar's Electronic Inquiry and General Information)** is a web-based registrar inquiry system designed to make registrar-related information easier for students to access.

The system provides a centralized platform where students can view **registrar announcements, frequently asked questions, procedures, and general information** without needing to visit the registrar's office for every inquiry.

REIGI also includes a **kiosk-integrated setup** to provide students with another way to access registrar information on campus.

---

## 🔴 `> PROBLEM`

Students often need to visit or contact the Registrar's Office just to ask common questions or check announcements.

This can result in:

- Repeated inquiries
- Long waiting times
- Difficulty accessing registrar information
- Repetitive work for registrar staff
- Students missing important announcements

REIGI was developed to provide a more accessible and centralized solution.

---

## 🔴 `> OBJECTIVES`

REIGI aims to:

- Provide centralized registrar announcements
- Provide frequently asked questions and answers
- Make registrar information easier to access
- Reduce repetitive inquiries
- Improve communication between students and the Registrar's Office
- Provide an alternative kiosk-based access point
- Help registrar staff manage and update information

---

## 🔴 `> KEY FEATURES`

### 📢 Announcements

Allows students to view important announcements and updates from the Registrar's Office.

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Provides answers to common registrar-related questions and inquiries.

### 🏫 General Information

Provides useful information about registrar services, procedures, and requirements.

### 🖥️ Kiosk Integration

Designed to support access through a kiosk within the campus.

### 🔐 Admin Management

Registrar administrators can manage announcements, FAQs, and system information.

### 🎙️ Voice Automation

Provides voice-based interaction to improve accessibility and user experience.

### 🛡️ Bot Protection

Uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help protect the system from automated abuse.
